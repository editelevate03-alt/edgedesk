    Playbook: how to use EdgeDesk

    1. Check once or twice a day

    Indian markets: after 3:40 PM IST, when the daily candle is finished. Forex & gold: after the new day starts (5:30 AM IST). Signals use finished daily candles, so there's no need to watch every minute.

    2. A signal arrives (Telegram + this page)

    • Check the score (60+ only) and the backtest verdict (must be TRADEABLE).
    • Note the entry, stop and target. Never trade without the stop.
    • Use the size shown. It risks 1% of the paper account. If 1 lot is more than 1%, skip the trade.
    • If price has already run far past the entry, skip it. Don't chase. The guard blocks this too.

    3. While in a trade

    • Don't move the stop further away. Let the stop or target do the work.
    • The forward-test bot closes the paper trade at the stop, the target or the time limit, and sends a Telegram message.

    4. Safety rules (built in)

    • 1% risk per trade · max 5 open trades · max 2 same-direction trades in related markets.
    • Daily loss limit: after −3R of closed losses in a day, no new signals until tomorrow.
    • The mistake guard blocks: no stop, tight or wide stops, R:R below 1.5, unproven strategies, chasing, stale data, duplicates and conflicting signals.

    5. Be honest with yourself

    Paper trade for at least 1–3 months. Compare the forward-test record with the backtest. If live results are much worse, stop and review. Losing streaks of 5–10 trades are normal even for strategies with an edge.
